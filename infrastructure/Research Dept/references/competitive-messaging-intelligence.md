# Competitive Messaging Intelligence

Use this extension when the user asks what competitors publicly say, sell, advertise, promise, prove, or repeat across ads, landing pages, social posts, product pages, case studies, and marketplaces.

The output is factual messaging intelligence, not strategy advice. It can say "competitors repeatedly emphasize X" when the evidence supports it. It must not say "Anton should use X" unless the user explicitly asks for a separate strategic handoff.

## Trigger phrases

- competitor ads
- реклама конкурентов
- offers
- офферы
- CTA
- landing-page messaging
- positioning
- creative patterns
- sales narrative
- value proposition map
- ad library
- Google Ads Transparency Center
- Meta Ads Library
- TikTok Creative Center

## Required brief additions

- Competitors or category boundaries.
- Geography and language.
- Channels to inspect.
- Time horizon and freshness requirement.
- Whether paid ads, organic social, landing pages, or all public messaging are in scope.
- Whether screenshots/captures are required.
- Whether the output is for factual understanding only or a later strategy handoff.

## Source mix

Prefer:

- official ad transparency libraries when accessible;
- competitor landing pages and product/pricing pages;
- case studies and customer story pages;
- public social posts and profiles;
- search-result ad snippets where observable;
- marketplaces, app stores, review pages, and directory profiles;
- archived captures when current pages are volatile;
- reputable trade commentary only as secondary context.

Do not treat ad presence as performance proof. Ads prove that a message was publicly visible, not that it worked.

## Required artifacts

Follow the normal department chain, and add:

`Messaging Corpus Plan -> Competitive Message Map`

Use `templates/competitive-message-map.md` for the message map.

## Dossier sections

- Scope and source coverage.
- Competitor/channel coverage table.
- Repeated pains.
- Repeated offers.
- Repeated CTAs.
- Repeated proof types.
- Landing-page structure patterns.
- Creative/message format patterns.
- Contradictions and outliers.
- What is directly observed.
- What is inferred.
- Unknowns and missing channels.
- Evidence anchors.

## Quality gates

Fail the run if:

- the report claims a message works without performance evidence;
- inferred strategy is not separated from observed copy/creative;
- date-sensitive ad/page evidence has no capture or observation date;
- channel coverage is too narrow for the user's requested scope;
- screenshots or capture notes were required but are absent.
