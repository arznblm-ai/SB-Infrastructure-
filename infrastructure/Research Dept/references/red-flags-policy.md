# Red Flags Policy

### [[2026-04-14]]

## Mandatory red flags

1. The research relies mainly on generic SEO content.
2. Most sources are derivative summaries.
3. Key claims are supported only by one weak source.
4. No primary source was used despite being available.
5. Research repeats one dominant narrative with no opposition.
6. The strongest sources are too old for the topic.
7. The newest sources are all weak-signal and hype-driven.
8. Conclusions are stronger than the evidence.
9. Source provenance is unclear.
10. The final synthesis hides uncertainty.

## Critic response

If any red flag is found, the critic must:

- name the red flag explicitly;
- explain why it weakens reliability;
- require revision;
- specify what additional search or verification is needed.

## Router pre-checks

Router should catch early:

- requests too broad for quality coverage;
- requests asking for high confidence with low-quality available source mix;
- weak-signal topics presented as if they were stable factual domains.
