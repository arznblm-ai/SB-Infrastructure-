# Account / ICP Intelligence

### [[2026-06-07]]

Use this extension when the user asks for factual lead, account, persona, or ICP research. The goal is to map who appears to fit and why, using public evidence. It is not a replacement for CRM data, private enrichment, or outreach strategy.

## Trigger phrases

- ICP
- lead research
- target accounts
- buyer personas
- account qualification
- prospect list
- похожие компании
- кто покупатель
- какие компании подходят
- public account intelligence

## Required brief additions

- Product, offer, or category being matched.
- Geography and company size boundaries.
- Buyer role/persona assumptions.
- Inclusion criteria.
- Exclusion criteria.
- Required public signals.
- Whether individual people are in scope.
- Privacy and source limits.
- Output format: segment map, account table, persona map, or CEO report.

## Source mix

Prefer:

- company websites and product pages;
- public customer pages and case studies;
- hiring pages and role openings;
- funding/news/public filings where relevant;
- marketplace and review footprints;
- LinkedIn/company pages when accessible;
- public directories and databases;
- technology footprint only when sourceable and current.

Avoid:

- fabricated contact details;
- private personal data;
- confidence claims based on a single weak signal;
- treating funding, hiring, or tech stack as proof of buying intent.

## Required artifacts

Follow the normal department chain, and add:

`ICP Hypothesis -> Account / ICP Map`

Use `templates/account-icp-map.md` for the account/ICP map.

## ICP map fields

- Segment.
- Account or persona.
- Public fit signals.
- Disqualifying signals.
- Buyer role evidence.
- Trigger or urgency signal.
- Budget/scale proxy.
- Source links.
- Confidence.
- Missing data.
- Notes.

## Dossier sections

- ICP hypothesis.
- Inclusion and exclusion rules.
- Segment map.
- Account/persona evidence table.
- Fit signals by strength.
- Disqualifying signals.
- Trigger events and timing.
- Buyer-role evidence.
- Data gaps and no-contact constraints.
- Evidence anchors.

## Quality gates

Fail the run if:

- it produces a prospect recommendation without public evidence;
- confidence labels are missing;
- account fit is based on one weak proxy;
- personal/private data is fabricated or inferred;
- missing data before outreach/prioritization is hidden.
