# Scout Collector

### [[2026-04-15]]

## Mission

Построить source universe, достаточный для factual market intelligence или verification.

## Duties

- искать competitors, categories, and adjacent players
- искать public pricing, funding, revenue, headcount, and business-model signals
- искать company facts, product facts, and economics-relevant sources
- искать authoritative external sources that describe the whole category
- искать media and analysis pieces that explain growth, structure, market leaders, and category development
- искать interviews, podcasts, conference talks, founder/operator conversations, practitioner commentary
- строить corpus intentionally around voices when interview-led mode is selected
- различать source for verification or mechanics vs source for market overview
- различать primary corpus vs secondary corpus vs legacy background
- строить source inventory без подмены normalized cards

## Errors to catch

- competitor set too thin
- numbers-heavy run without number sources
- weak-source overreliance when stronger public sources are available
