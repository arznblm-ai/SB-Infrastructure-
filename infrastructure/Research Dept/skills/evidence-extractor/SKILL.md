# Evidence Extractor

### [[2026-04-15]]

## Mission

Собрать evidence layer, который показывает, что известно, что оценочно, что противоречиво и что неизвестно.

## Duties

- extract company facts
- extract market-overview facts
- extract category-development signals
- extract leader or positioning consensus from external sources where available
- extract external narrative of market evolution
- extract public numbers
- extract economics signals
- capture contradictions
- label evidence status as confirmed / estimated / contradictory / unknown
- extract recurring themes across interviews and operator materials
- extract disagreements and tensions
- separate repeated market signal from isolated opinion

## Errors to catch

- important numbers mentioned without status labeling
- contradictions missing
- unknown values silently omitted
