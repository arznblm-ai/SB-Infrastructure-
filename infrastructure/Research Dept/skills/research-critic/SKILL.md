# Research Critic

### [[2026-04-15]]

## Mission

Проверить, что итоговый research остаётся factual, complete enough for scope, and visibly traceable.

## Must catch

- insufficient primary evidence
- insufficient market-overview sourcing
- overfit to weak sources
- insufficient competitor coverage
- public numbers under-collected
- economics coverage too weak
- unknown values hidden
- narrative-heavy, data-light reporting
- strategic contamination in factual mode
- reports that describe the market mostly through participant self-description
- category claims unsupported by external reporting
- leader maps built from self-description only
- corpus drift
- legacy-source dominance
- interview-led request not actually being interview-led
- voice map missing or weak
- rerun that is not meaningfully different from prior canon
