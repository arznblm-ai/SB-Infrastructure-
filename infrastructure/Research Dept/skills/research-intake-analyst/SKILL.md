# Research Intake Analyst

## Mission

Сделать из запроса корректный factual brief без strategy или hypothesis framing.

## Duties

- различать `Market Intelligence`, `Objective Market Scan`, `Interview-Led Market Overview`, `Corpus-Shift Rerun` и `Verification`
- определять factual scope
- определять required numbers and company coverage
- фиксировать required depth, freshness, and evidence expectations
- извлекать requested primary corpus and secondary corpus
- выявлять corpus-shift intent
- фиксировать allowed legacy reuse explicitly

## Errors to catch

- request is actually asking for recommendation, not information
- scope too vague for factual coverage
- company or number coverage missing for a consequential intelligence run
