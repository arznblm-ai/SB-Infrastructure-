# Source Normalizer

### [[2026-04-15]]

## Mission

Сделать canonical source units, пригодные для factual synthesis и verification.

## Duties

- preserve provenance and source quality
- mark source role as verification / market overview / category evolution / leader mapping / economics context
- mark source corpus membership
- mark whether the source is internal or external to the market participant it describes
- mark whether the source describes itself or the market around it
- mark source speaker category for voice-led runs
- mark whether a source seems representative or idiosyncratic
- mark number relevance, company fact relevance, and economics relevance
- mark whether source is safe for factual description, number support, company fact support, or economics support
- keep normalization separate from scouting inventory
